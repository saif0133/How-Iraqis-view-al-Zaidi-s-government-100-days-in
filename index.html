IIACSS’s 100 Days Survey examines how Iraqis assess the al-Zaidi government during its first months in office. Based on a nationally representative survey of 2,056 Iraqis, the findings show strong early optimism, with 77% expressing a positive view of the government. Yet concerns over corruption, unemployment, and other long-standing challenges remain central to public opinion.

Join us for a discussion of the findings, exploring what Iraqis think of the new government and whether early optimism can translate into lasting trust.

The discussion is hosted by the Atlantic Council and will examine how Iraqis evaluate the government’s performance so far, which issues are shaping public opinion, and what these early attitudes may mean for confidence in the government over the months ahead.
